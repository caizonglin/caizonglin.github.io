Features

Everything you need for embodied AI research and development.

Low-Cost Full Open Source

Complete CAD files, firmware, and code open-sourced for free. Estimated BOM of only ≈ ¥5,718 RMB — add an optional Raspberry Pi or Jetson.

7+1-DOF Humanoid Arms + Grippers

Humanoid-form arms with 7 degrees of freedom plus integrated grippers, driven by digital serial-bus servos for dexterous manipulation.

Dual-Mode Mobile Base

Switchable two-wheel differential or four-wheel all-wheel-drive locomotion using digital wheel servos.

Leadscrew Lift (Self-Locking)

Leadscrew-based lift with a wide travel range. Self-locking design ensures zero-persistence — no re-homing needed.

2-DOF Head Pan/Tilt

Head unit with 2 degrees of freedom — pan and tilt — for dynamic camera positioning and environmental awareness.

Vision Pipeline (LeRobot ACT / SmolVLA)

Full vision pipeline supporting LeRobot ACT and SmolVLA policies. 3–4× 720p cameras for rich spatial perception.

Dual-Machine Teleop & Training

ZMQ-based architecture for teleoperation, data collection, training, and real-time inference — works in both single-machine and dual-machine modes. Display via Rerun / Foxglove.

Vision-Based Navigation

Camera-based autonomous navigation for obstacle avoidance and goal reaching without extra sensors.
